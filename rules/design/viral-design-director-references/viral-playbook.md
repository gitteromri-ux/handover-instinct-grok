# Viral Playbook — Hooks, Composition & Platform Specs

The mechanics behind work that spreads. Read when setting direction (Step 2) or producing social/ad/thumbnail work.

## The Viral Hook — pick exactly one per piece

A "hook" is the single unexpected move that makes a feed-scroller stop. Overusing hooks creates noise; one sharp hook reads as intentional and premium.

- **Scale jump** — one word, object, or element blown massively out of proportion to everything else.
- **Negative-space reveal** — the subject is partly formed by the empty space; the eye completes it.
- **Impossible depth** — an element breaks the frame / pops through a layer / casts a shadow onto the UI itself.
- **Color isolation** — one saturated accent in an otherwise restrained or monochrome field.
- **Pattern interrupt** — a grid/repetition with exactly one element broken, rotated, or replaced.
- **Tactile realism** — hyper-real material, lighting, and shadow on an otherwise graphic composition (the "is this 3D or a photo?" effect).
- **Type as image** — the headline IS the composition; letterforms are sculpted, layered, or lit.

## Composition fundamentals

- **One focal point.** Establish it in the first 0.5s. Everything else is hierarchy beneath it.
- **Rule-of-thirds or dead-center, never timid off-center.** Commit.
- **Generous breathing room.** Premium = confident negative space, not crammed.
- **Z-axis hierarchy.** Foreground / midground / background must be distinct via scale, blur, and shadow.
- **Edge tension.** Let elements kiss or cross edges intentionally; avoid floating-in-a-void unless that's the concept.
- **Optical alignment > mathematical alignment.** Trust the eye for final nudges.

## Platform specs & format defaults

| Use case | Aspect / size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IG feed post (portrait) | 1080×1350 (4:5) | Default for max feed real estate |
| IG square | 1080×1080 (1:1) | Carousels, grid-cohesion |
| IG Story / Reel / TikTok | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Keep key content out of top/bottom 250px (UI overlap) |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280×720 (16:9) | 1 face or 1 object + ≤3 words, high contrast, readable at 120px wide |
| Landing hero | 16:9 / full-bleed responsive | Above-the-fold = hero idea + single CTA |
| X / LinkedIn image | 1200×675 (16:9) | Legible at small inline size |

## Social / thumbnail rules

- **Readable at thumbnail size.** If the headline isn't legible scaled to 120px wide, it fails.
- **≤ 5 words on a thumbnail; ≤ 7 on an ad.** Text is a graphic element, not a paragraph.
- **Faces & eyes pull engagement** — when on-brand, use a strong gaze line toward the headline or CTA.
- **Contrast is king.** A scroll-stopper has a clear light-vs-dark or saturated-vs-neutral split.

## Video / Reel direction

- **0–1s hook frame** — open on the single most striking image; no slow build, no logo-first intros.
- **Beat structure** — Hook → tension/build → payoff → CTA. Keep each beat tight.
- **Type animation** — kinetic typography that enters with weight (scale-up + slight overshoot + settle), synced to audio beats.
- **Pacing** — cut on the beat; faster cuts in the hook, breathing room at the payoff.
- **Loop-ability** — design the last frame to flow into the first for seamless replays.
